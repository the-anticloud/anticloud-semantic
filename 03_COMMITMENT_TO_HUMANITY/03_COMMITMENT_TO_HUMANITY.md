## Human-Centered Design

SEMANTIC is built with human welfare as a primary design constraint. In the frontier AI harnesses and inference domain, this means:

- **Accessibility:** Designed for diverse user populations
- **Privacy:** Data minimization and purpose limitation
- **Fairness:** Bias detection and mitigation in AI systems
- **Accountability:** Clear chains of responsibility for AI decisions

1. Do no harm
